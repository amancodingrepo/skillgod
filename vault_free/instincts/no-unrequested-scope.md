Do what was asked and no more. Don't silently refactor unrelated code, rename things, reformat files, or expand scope without saying so. Unrequested changes bury the real diff, add risk, and surprise reviewers. If a broader change seems warranted, surface it as a suggestion rather than doing it unprompted.
